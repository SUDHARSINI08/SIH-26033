## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AGROBRIDGE 360 – AI-POWERED SMART AGRICULTURAL ECOSYSTEM

Project Vision

AGROBRIDGE 360 is a next-generation intelligent agricultural marketplace designed to transform the traditional agricultural supply chain into a transparent, efficient and technology-driven ecosystem.

Instead of simply connecting farmers and consumers, the platform intelligently predicts market demand, recommends fair prices, connects nearby buyers, coordinates transportation and identifies opportunities to reduce food wastage.

It creates a direct digital bridge between farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), retailers, supermarkets, restaurants and consumers.

Revolutionary Features

1. AI HARVEST INTELLIGENCE

An intelligent prediction system that analyzes seasonal patterns, historical market prices and demand trends to help farmers understand what products may have higher market demand.

2. FAIRPRICE AI – Intelligent Price Recommendation

Analyzes available market information, demand, supply and product availability to recommend competitive selling prices. It helps farmers make informed pricing decisions rather than depending entirely on intermediaries.

3. SMARTBUY – Collective Purchasing Network

Allows multiple consumers, restaurants and retailers in nearby locations to combine their requirements and purchase directly from farmers. This creates larger orders and potentially reduces delivery expenses.

4. ROUTEGENIUS – Intelligent Logistics

Uses location and order information to identify suitable delivery routes and coordinate multiple deliveries, reducing unnecessary transportation and delays.

5. HARVEST ALERT – Surplus Rescue Network

Identifies agricultural products with excess available stock and sends alerts to nearby potential buyers. This helps connect surplus produce with demand before it loses market value.

6. TRUSTTRACE – Digital Product Journey

Maintains digital information about a product, including farmer details, harvest date, quantity, quality information and transaction history, improving transparency between sellers and buyers.

7. VOICEFARM – Regional Language Assistant

Provides voice-based interaction in Tamil, English and other regional languages so farmers can access important platform features without complicated typing.

8. MARKET PULSE – Live Agricultural Insights

Provides an analytical dashboard showing product availability, price trends, buyer demand and sales information to support better agricultural decisions.

Our Innovation

AGROBRIDGE 360 combines predictive intelligence, collective buying, surplus redistribution, transparent product information and logistics optimization into one unified agricultural ecosystem.

Its central innovation is not merely connecting two parties but intelligently coordinating the entire journey from harvest to consumer.

System Architecture:
Layer 1 – User Interface

Separate dashboards for farmers, buyers, administrators and logistics providers.

Layer 2 – Application Backend

Handles registration, product listings, order management, transactions and notifications.

Layer 3 – AI Intelligence Engine

Processes historical and current market information to generate price recommendations, demand forecasts and delivery suggestions.

Layer 4 – Database Management

Stores user profiles, agricultural products, orders, market information and transaction records.

Layer 5 – Logistics Integration

Connects orders with available transportation services and provides delivery updates.

Working Methodology:
1.Farmers register and create digital product listings.

2.Product details and available quantities are uploaded.

3.AI analyzes market trends and generates price recommendations.

4.Buyers discover products based on location, quantity and price.

5.SmartBuy combines nearby buyer requirements.

6.RouteGenius recommends efficient delivery arrangements.

7.TrustTrace maintains product and transaction information.

8.The system collects transaction data to improve future predictions.

System Workflow
FARMER → PRODUCT REGISTRATION → AI MARKET ANALYSIS → FAIR PRICE RECOMMENDATION → BUYER MATCHING → COLLECTIVE ORDERING → SMART LOGISTICS → DELIVERY → PAYMENT.

## Feasibility and Viability
Technical Feasibility:
The proposed platform can be developed using accessible open-source technologies and existing mapping and payment services.

An initial prototype can be implemented with sample market datasets, basic machine learning models and simulated delivery information.

Economic Viability:
1.Reduces unnecessary intermediary-related expenses.

2.Creates additional market access for small farmers.

3.Supports affordable digital infrastructure.

4.Provides opportunities for partnerships with FPOs and logistics providers.

5.Can be expanded through a scalable cloud-based architecture.

Potential Challenges:
1.Limited digital literacy among rural farmers.

2.Inaccurate or incomplete market information.

3.Transportation and storage limitations.

4.Maintaining product quality and trust.

5.Internet connectivity challenges.

Risk Management Strategies:
1.Multilingual interfaces and voice assistance.

2.Reliable data sources and regular updates.

3.Partnerships with local FPOs and transport providers.

4.Transparent product information and verified accounts.

5.Lightweight mobile interfaces with offline draft capabilities.

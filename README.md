## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmConnect is an intelligent digital agricultural marketplace designed to bridge the gap between farmers and consumers by eliminating unnecessary intermediaries.
Unlike traditional agricultural marketplaces, FarmConnect combines direct product selling, AI-powered demand prediction, smart logistics, and transparent pricing in one platform.

Innovative Features:
1.Direct Deal System: Farmers can sell their products directly to households, supermarkets, restaurants, retailers, and bulk buyers.

2.AI Price Intelligence: An intelligent pricing system analyzes market trends, product availability, and demand to help farmers make informed pricing decisions.

3.Smart Demand Prediction: AI predicts future demand based on historical sales, seasonal patterns, and market trends, helping farmers plan their produce.

4. Logistics Network: Connects farmers with nearby delivery partners and identifies efficient transportation routes.

5.Group Buying: Multiple consumers can combine their orders to purchase agricultural products at more affordable prices.

6.Farm Fresh Tracking: Buyers can view product origin, farmer details, harvesting information, and delivery status.

7.Multilingual Voice Assistance: Farmers can interact with the platform using regional languages and voice commands.

## Technical Approach
FarmConnect uses web development, Artificial Intelligence, database management, and mapping technologies to create a smart agricultural ecosystem.

Technologies:
1.Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js.

2.Backend: Python Flask.

3.Database: MySQL.

4.Artificial Intelligence: Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas.

5.Maps: Google Maps API.

6.Payment: UPI integration.

## Feasibility and Viability
FarmConnect is practical to implement because it uses affordable technologies and existing digital infrastructure.

Technical Feasibility:
1.Built using accessible and open-source technologies.

2.Can operate through smartphones and computers.

3.Cloud infrastructure allows future expansion.

4.AI features can initially be implemented using basic machine-learning models.

Economic Feasibility:
1.Reduces unnecessary intermediary commissions.

2. farmers reach more buyers.

3.Reduces delivery expenses through route optimization.

4.Creates opportunities for local transport providers.
